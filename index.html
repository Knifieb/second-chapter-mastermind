Questions before you join?

Frequently asked questions

What if I’m not in TikTok Shop yet?

You can still join. The Building My Way In starting point will help you work toward your first 1,000 followers, prepare your page and understand what comes next.

What if I’m already making sales?

You belong in Scaling What Sells. We’ll focus on analytics, winning products, repeatable content, campaigns, seller bonuses and holiday growth opportunities.

What if I’m inside the Creator Pilot Program?

We’ll help you understand the program, protect your account, work within the current limitations and build toward the requirements shown inside your account.

Do I have to attend every call live?

No. Calls will be recorded and placed in the community, although attending live gives you the opportunity to ask questions and receive coaching in real time.

Does the program guarantee followers, samples or income?

No. TikTok controls platform access, eligibility and distribution, and individual results vary. You’ll receive strategy, education, feedback and support, but you are responsible for completing the work and following current platform policies.
